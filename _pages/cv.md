### Incremental Hierarchical Semantic Scene Graphs (INHerit-SG) (2025.10 -- 2026.02)
*Advisors: Prof. Jieqi Shi, Prof. Hao Xu, Prof. Jing Huo*

*   First author and project lead; led the end-to-end system development, experiment design, manuscript preparation, and real-robot evaluation.
*   Introduced an online Floor-Room-Area-Object hierarchy with RAG-style retrieval, natural-language semantic anchors, and event-triggered updates for long-term consistency.
*   Constructed HM3DSem-SQR and a human-study dataset for complex natural-language queries and semantic evaluation.
*   Achieved 37.7% geometric accuracy (within 1 m), 70.6% semantic accuracy, and 60.0% real-world trajectory success; selected for an ICRA 2026 Workshop oral and named a Best Presentation Finalist.

### Zero-Shot Vision-and-Language Navigation (LaViRA) (2025.05 -- 2025.09)
*Advisors: Prof. Jieqi Shi, Prof. Jing Huo*

*   Developed a coarse-to-fine hierarchy that translates language plans into visually grounded actions and precise robot controls for zero-shot VLN-CE.
*   Achieved 38.3% Success Rate and 28.3% SPL with Gemini-2.5-Pro, improving over InstructNav by 16.1 and 17.7 percentage points, respectively.
*   Led real-robot deployment and system integration on Unitree Go1 and AgileX Cobot Magic; accepted at ICRA 2026.

### Active Exploration with Semantic Map Prediction (SEA) (2025.09 -- 2025.10)
*Advisors: Prof. Jieqi Shi, Prof. Jing Huo*

*   Developed an iterative prediction-exploration framework with confidence-aware semantic map updates and hierarchical navigation policies.
*   Deployed and debugged the full Habitat pipeline with real-robot integration on Unitree Go1 and AgileX Cobot Magic.
